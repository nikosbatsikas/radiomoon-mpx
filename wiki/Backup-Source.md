# Backup Source (Receiver)

Input → **Backup source** (window) or *Backup* (web page): when the stream stops, the MPX of a 192 kHz sound card input goes on air — an analog STL, an off-air receiver or a local processor.

- *Switch to backup after* (default 1000 ms without stream) — also at start when no stream has come yet.
- *Back to the stream after* (default 5 s of steady stream).
- *Also on silence*: switch when the stream carries silence (MPX below −40 dB).
- Level in dB, left / right channel. The input is recorded all the time; 10 ms fade-in.

The header shows **ON BACKUP**; each switch is in the [[Receiver Log]]. A switch is a hard cut of the composite (a short click on air is possible).
